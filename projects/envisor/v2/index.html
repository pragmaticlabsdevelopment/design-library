Our Mission

Environmental review should be
faster, not weaker.

For decades, environmental review has been defined by its burdens: hundreds of documents to parse, thousands of comments to answer, and a regulatory framework that demands precision at every turn. The result is a process that often takes years, costs millions, and still faces legal challenge.

Envisor was built to change that. We develop purpose-built AI that understands CEQA, NEPA, and environmental compliance from the ground up — not general-purpose tools repurposed for regulatory work, but systems designed for the specific demands of defensible environmental review.

The Challenge

A process built for paper
in a world that moves at scale.

01

Regulatory Complexity

CEQA, NEPA, state equivalents, agency protocols, technical thresholds — the regulatory landscape is fragmented and always evolving.

02

Extended Timelines

Major environmental reviews routinely take 2–5 years. Infrastructure projects stall. Housing gets delayed. Costs compound.

03

Defensibility Burden

Every finding, every response, every mitigation measure must hold up to agency review, public scrutiny, and legal challenge.

What We Do

Three ways Envisor transforms environmental review.

01

Accelerate Document Review

Our AI reads, organizes, and cross-references environmental documents — EIRs, MNDs, technical studies, comment letters — so your team can focus on analysis and judgment, not searching through PDFs.

  • Automated document parsing and classification
  • Cross-reference identification across the record
  • Threshold and regulatory mapping
02

Strengthen Defensibility

Every output Envisor generates is tied to source material in the project record. Responses cite statutes, thresholds, and evidence. The audit trail is built in, not bolted on.

  • Source-backed responses and findings
  • Full administrative record traceability
  • Citation-linked comment responses
03

Scale Without Compromise

Handle more projects, more comments, more complexity — without proportionally scaling headcount. Envisor augments your team's capacity while preserving the rigor that defines quality environmental work.

  • 2–3x capacity per environmental professional
  • 50%+ reduction in review timelines
  • Consistent quality across projects
