How it works

AI-powered upscaling

This tool uses Real-ESRGAN, a state-of-the-art super-resolution model, to upscale your images 2x. It's especially good at sharpening AI-generated images, screenshots, and artwork.

Runs in your browser

Everything happens on your device using WebGPU acceleration. Your images never leave your computer — nothing is uploaded to any server.

Limits

Supports PNG, JPEG, and WebP images up to 15 MB and 5000px per side. The AI model (~67 MB) downloads once and is cached in your browser.
