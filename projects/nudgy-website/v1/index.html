Questions

FAQ

What is Nudgy?

Nudgy is a lightweight Mac screenshot tool for capturing, annotating, and sharing visual feedback. Grab any part of your screen, mark it up with arrows, shapes, blur, highlights, and text, then paste it into AI tools, Slack, Jira, GitHub, or wherever work happens.

It's built for speed. One shortcut to capture, a few clicks to annotate, and you're done.

Who is Nudgy for?

Nudgy is for vibe coders, product managers, designers, developers, support teams, sales engineers, and anyone in tech who needs to explain what's on screen quickly.

If you've ever spent three paragraphs describing a UI bug you could have just circled, Nudgy is for you.

Can I use Nudgy with AI tools?

Yes. Nudgy is useful for giving Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and similar tools better visual context. Capture the screenshot, mark what matters, and paste it into your AI workflow.

Annotated screenshots remove ambiguity from prompts. AI models understand images natively — a marked-up screenshot with an arrow pointing at a bug gives far more context than a paragraph trying to describe it.

How is Nudgy different from a normal screenshot?

A normal screenshot shows the screen. Nudgy helps you show what matters.

With arrows, circles, highlights, blur, and text labels, you can point attention exactly where it needs to go. The result is a screenshot that actually communicates — not just a raw capture that requires a paragraph of explanation alongside it.

Why not just use built-in macOS screenshots?

macOS gives you fullscreen or selected area — that's it. No annotation, no scrolling capture. The moment you need to circle a bug or add context, you're opening Preview and losing your flow.

Nudgy also does scrolling capture — something macOS can't do at all. Grab an entire Slack thread, a 20-page PRD, a lengthy DocuSign contract, or a full error log in one shot. Paste it into AI and get a summary instead of reading hundreds of pages. That's huge for generating tickets from context, digesting long documents, or giving AI the full picture instead of a cropped fragment.

Capture, annotate, paste — one step, not three apps.

Does anything get uploaded or sent to a server?

No. Nudgy runs entirely on your Mac. No cloud, no server, no analytics, no telemetry. Your screenshots never leave your machine unless you explicitly choose to share them.

This matters especially if you're working on proprietary code, internal tools, or client projects. Your visual context stays private.

How fast is the workflow?

A couple seconds. For vibe coding workflows where you just need to show AI what's on screen, it's virtually instant — shortcut, drag, paste.

If you're adding annotations like arrows or highlights, you're still done in a few seconds. The global shortcut triggers from anywhere, so you never leave the app you're working in.

Is it really free?

The core product is free forever. Capture, annotate, save locally — no limits on that for 25 screenshots per month, no account required.

Pro tiers unlock unlimited screenshots, shareable links, animated GIFs, and cloud storage for teams that need more. But the free version is a fully capable tool, not a trial.
